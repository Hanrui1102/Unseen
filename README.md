# UNSEEN

*An interactive digital museum and support space for eating disorder awareness.*

UNSEEN brings together watercolor storytelling, interactive exhibits, and practical tools to help visitors understand eating disorders, express their needs, and explore support options.

[Visit the website](https://incandescent-douhua-9bcac8.netlify.app/)

## Explore the museum

- **Behind the Label** — Reveal misconceptions about appearance, willpower, restrictive dieting, and binge eating.
- **The Language Room** — Explore everyday situations and practice supportive responses.
- **Unsaid** — Discover fictional reflections about uncertainty, being seen, and asking for help.

Visitors can explore rooms in any order and access support tools directly.

## Support tools

- **Find Your Way** — Choose a current concern and explore a possible next step.
- **Letter Studio** — Create and edit a message to a friend, family member, school counselor, or healthcare professional.
- **Appointment Desk** — Prepare questions for a first appointment and create a visit card.
- **Support Atlas** — Browse educational resources, treatment directories, and external support groups.
- **Loved Ones Guide** — Explore ways to start a conversation and offer practical support.

## GLP-1 research tracking

UNSEEN currently links to an external educational resource about GLP-1 medicines and eating disorders. A dedicated research tracker is planned for a future release.

The tracker will explore GLP-1 medicines in the context of eating disorders and eating-related concerns through:

- **Research updates** — Summaries of relevant studies, including their design, participants, findings, and limitations.
- **Clinical guidance** — Updates from professional organizations and health authorities.
- **Evidence status** — Clear distinctions between preliminary findings, established guidance, and unanswered questions.
- **Source transparency** — Links to original publications, publication dates, and the date each entry was last reviewed.
- **Appointment preparation** — Questions visitors can bring to a qualified healthcare professional when discussing these medicines.

Content will focus on understanding evidence and preparing for informed conversations with healthcare professionals. Clinical summaries will be marked with their review status.

## Visual design

The website combines watercolor imagery, paper textures, hand-drawn icons, and soft purple tones.

A watercolor opening introduces the experience. Interactive rooms incorporate water-lily imagery, gentle light, and paper-inspired controls.

A vintage balance illustration, with petals on one pan and a heart on the other, carries the message—

**Don’t let your weight define your worth.**

## Open locally

1. Download `UNSEEN. — Eating disorder support.html` from this repository.
2. Open the downloaded file in a modern web browser.

The HTML file includes the website’s styles, scripts, fonts, and images. External resource links require an internet connection.

## Privacy and accessibility

Support tools work in the browser without an account. Drafts are held in the current page, while copied or downloaded text may remain on the visitor’s device.

The website includes a skip control for the opening, keyboard-accessible controls, and support for reduced-motion preferences.

## Project status

UNSEEN is a publicly available early version. Clinical content is pending professional review. Further mobile, accessibility, and usability testing is planned.

The live website and the downloadable HTML file may reflect different stages of development.

UNSEEN provides education and preparation for seeking support. For diagnosis or treatment, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## Development roadmap

- Obtain professional review of educational content and communication templates.
- Test the website across mobile devices and browsers.
- Improve keyboard navigation and screen-reader support through accessibility testing.
- Refine the museum experience through visitor feedback.
- Expand regional support resources and regularly review external links.
- Develop the GLP-1 research tracker with dated entries, original sources, and review status.
